Parent User Guide

Silver Oak Juniors  ·  oakit.silveroakjuniors.in

Role: Parent  —  Stay connected with your child's school day

1. What is Oakit.ai for Parents?

Oakit.ai is your school's parent communication and updates app. Through Oakit, you can see everything happening in your child's school day without calling the school or messaging teachers on WhatsApp.

You can seeYou cannot change
Daily photos and videos from classAttendance records
Your child's attendance statusCurriculum or lesson plans
Homework assigned by the teacherOther children's information
Curriculum coverage progressFee amounts or structures
Milestones your child has achievedTeacher or admin accounts
Holistic term reports (AI-generated)
Fee invoices and payment history
Messages from the teacher
School announcements and updates

2. Logging In

1
Open https://oakit.silveroakjuniors.in in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
2
Enter your mobile number (the one registered with the school) and your password.
3
Tap Sign In. The app opens to your child's home view.
If you cannot log in

Your account is set up by the school admin. If you are logging in for the first time and the school gave you a temporary password, that password is usually your mobile number. Contact the school if you are still unable to log in.

2.1 Changing Your Password

1
Log in → tap the Settings tab (gear icon).
2
Tap Change Password.
3
Enter your current password, then your new password twice. Use at least 8 characters.

3. Installing the App on Your Phone

Oakit works as a web app — you do not need to download it from an app store. But you can install it on your home screen for quick access, just like a regular app.

3.1 On Android (Chrome)

1
Open https://oakit.silveroakjuniors.in in Chrome.
2
Tap the three-dot menu (top right of Chrome).
3
Tap Add to Home Screen. Confirm by tapping Add.
4
The Oakit icon appears on your home screen. Tap it to open the app anytime.

3.2 On iPhone (Safari)

1
Open https://oakit.silveroakjuniors.in in Safari (not Chrome — it must be Safari on iPhone).
2
Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the box with an arrow pointing up).
3
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
4
Tap Add. The Oakit icon appears on your home screen.
Works offline too

Once installed, you can browse your child's feed, homework, and reports even without internet. The app shows the last loaded data until you reconnect.

4. First Time You Open the App

When you open Oakit for the first time (or until you complete the prompts), you may see these screens:

4.1 Instagram Follow Prompt

A colourful popup asks you to follow the school's Instagram page. Tap Open Instagram to follow the school. Once you have followed, tap I have followed — close. This popup will not appear again.

Following the school's Instagram means you will see all the great moments from school on your Instagram feed — photos, videos, and reels posted by teachers. It also helps the school reach other parents who might want to enrol their children.

4.2 Flash Message Popup

If the school has sent an important notice (e.g. a sudden holiday, a fee reminder, or a drive cleanup warning), you will see a full-screen message popup. Read it carefully and tap to close. It will not appear again once you have dismissed it.

5. Home Tab

The Home tab is your daily dashboard. It shows everything that matters about your child today.

SectionWhat it shows
AttendanceWhether your child is Present, Absent, or Late today. If Absent, you should have received a notification.
Today's topicWhat curriculum topic the teacher is covering today. You can discuss this with your child at home.
Feed postsRecent photos and videos the teacher posted from class today.
Homework dueAny homework that is due today or tomorrow.
Notifications / UpdatesNew messages from the teacher, school announcements, and milestone achievements.
Multiple children

If you have more than one child enrolled at the school, you will see a switcher at the top of the screen. Tap your child's name to switch between their views. Each child has completely separate information.

6. Class Feed

The Class Feed is the daily photo and video diary of your child's classroom. Teachers post 2–5 times per day showing activities, art projects, playground moments, and learning milestones.

6.1 Viewing Posts

  • Scroll through the feed to see all recent posts
  • Tap a photo to see it larger
  • Swipe left/right on a carousel of photos to see all images in that post
  • Tap the play button to watch a class video

6.2 Liking a Post

Tap the heart icon on any post to like it. The teacher sees how many parents have liked their posts — it encourages them to keep posting regularly.

6.3 Downloading Photos and Videos

Tap a photo → tap the download button (downward arrow icon). The photo saves to your phone's gallery.

Photos are only available temporarily

Feed photos are stored for 20–30 days (the school's retention period). After that, they are automatically removed from the feed. Download and save any photos you want to keep permanently. The school may also run a Drive Cleanup occasionally — see Section 15 for what to do when you receive that notice.

6.4 Sharing a Post to Instagram

Tap the share icon on a post → Share to Instagram. The app copies the caption and hashtags to your clipboard and opens Instagram. Paste the caption and tag the school when posting. This helps grow the school's social media presence and lets your friends see what your child is learning.

7. Attendance

Go to the Attendance tab to see your child's attendance history.

7.1 Today's Status

  • Present — your child was marked present by the teacher
  • Absent — your child was marked absent. You received a notification. If this is incorrect, contact the teacher through the Messages tab.
  • Late — your child arrived late
  • Not marked yet — the teacher has not submitted attendance yet today (usually by 9:30 AM)

7.2 Attendance History

The tab shows the last 30 days of attendance with a calendar view. You can see the attendance percentage and punctuality percentage.

What the percentage means

Attendance percentage = number of days present ÷ total school days × 100. If your child's attendance is below 75%, it may affect their learning continuity. The app shows a warning when attendance drops below the target.

8. Progress

Go to the Progress tab to see how much of the curriculum your child's class has covered.

  • Coverage percentage — what percentage of the textbook topics have been taught so far this term
  • By subject — if multiple subjects are uploaded, you can see coverage per subject
What this means for your child

A coverage percentage of 60% at mid-term means 60% of the planned topics have been completed. This is updated in real time as the teacher ticks off topics each day. If coverage is significantly behind, it means the class needs to catch up — the teacher and admin are aware of this.

9. Homework

Go to the Homework tab to see all assigned homework.

9.1 Viewing Homework

  • Each homework card shows: what to do, due date, and status (pending / acknowledged)
  • Tap a homework card to see full details

9.2 Acknowledging Homework

1
Tap the homework card to open it.
2
Once you have seen it and ensured your child will complete it, tap Acknowledge.
3
The teacher can see that you have acknowledged. This replaces the need for a physical homework diary signature.
Why acknowledging is important

When you tap Acknowledge, it confirms to the teacher that you are aware of the homework and have supported your child to complete it. Teachers can see which parents have not acknowledged, and may follow up with those families. It is the digital equivalent of signing the homework diary.

9.3 Homework History

Go to Homework → History tab to see all past homework assignments and your acknowledgement status for each.

10. Messages

Go to the Messages tab to communicate directly with your child's teacher.

1
Tap the Messages tab.
2
You will see conversation threads — one per teacher.
3
Tap a thread to open it. Type your message and tap Send.
Response times

Teachers are with students during school hours. Expect replies during breaks or after school hours (3–5 PM). For urgent matters, call the school office directly.

What to use messages for
  • Informing the teacher your child will be absent tomorrow
  • Asking about homework or class activities
  • Requesting a parent-teacher meeting
  • Sharing information the teacher should know (e.g. child is unwell today)

All messages are visible to the school admin. This ensures transparency and allows the school to monitor communication quality.

11. Milestones

Go to the Milestones tab to see developmental milestones your child has achieved.

11.1 What are Milestones?

Milestones are specific developmental achievements recorded by the teacher. They cover four areas:

  • Cognitive — thinking, problem-solving, counting, recognising letters
  • Physical — motor skills, coordination, drawing, cutting with scissors
  • Social — playing with others, sharing, following rules
  • Emotional — managing feelings, expressing emotions, independence

11.2 Reading the Milestones View

  • Achieved milestones are shown with a tick and the date they were recorded
  • Upcoming milestones (not yet achieved) may also be visible so you know what to work on at home
  • When a new milestone is recorded, you receive a notification
Use milestones to support learning at home

If you see a milestone your child has not achieved yet, you can practice it at home. For example, if "counts from 1 to 20 independently" is not yet ticked, spend 5 minutes counting with your child each evening.

12. Holistic Reports and Student Journey

Go to the Reports tab to access term reports and your child's learning journey.

12.1 Holistic Term Reports

At the end of each term, the teacher generates an AI-assisted holistic development report for your child. This is a paragraph-format report covering all four development areas based on the teacher's recorded milestones and observations.

  • Reports appear in the Reports tab when the teacher publishes them
  • You receive a notification when a new report is available
  • Tap Download PDF to save the report to your phone. Keep it — it is your child's developmental record.
These reports replace the paper term report

The holistic report in Oakit is the school's official developmental report for your child. It replaces the printed paper reports that schools traditionally send home. Download and save a copy after each term.

12.2 Student Journey

Go to Reports → Student Journey. This shows a timeline of your child's milestones, observations, and key moments from the academic year — a digital memory book of their growth.

13. Fees

Go to the Fees tab to view your child's fee account.

13.1 What You Can See

  • Invoice — the full fee structure for the academic year with all line items
  • Payment history — all payments recorded by the school with dates and amounts
  • Outstanding balance — how much is still due
  • Concessions applied — any discounts or concessions on your account

13.2 Making a Payment

Payments are recorded by the school admin when you pay in person or via bank transfer. The Oakit app does not process payments directly. After you pay the school, the payment appears in your Fees tab within 1–2 working days.

If your payment is not showing

Contact the school office. The admin records payments manually in the system. It may take a day for them to update the records after receiving your payment.

13.3 Downloading Fee Receipt

Go to Fees → Payment History → tap any payment → tap Download Receipt. The receipt saves as a PDF to your phone.

14. Settings and Preferences

Go to the Settings tab (gear icon at the bottom) to personalise your app experience.

14.1 Language Translation

Oakit can translate all teacher-written content into your language automatically.

1
Go to Settings → Translation Settings.
2
Turn on Enable Translation.
3
Select your language from the dropdown.
4
Feed posts, homework, and notes will now appear in your selected language.

Supported languages: Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil, Malayalam, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Punjabi, Urdu, Arabic, French, Spanish, and English.

14.2 Notification Preferences

Go to Settings → Notification Preferences. Turn individual notifications on or off:

  • Attendance updates (when teacher marks your child present/absent)
  • Homework assigned
  • New feed posts
  • New messages from teacher
  • Milestone achievements
  • Announcements

14.3 Emergency Contacts

Go to Settings → Emergency Contacts. Add or update emergency contact details for your child. These are visible to the school admin and teacher in case of an emergency.

14.4 Profile Photo

Tap your profile photo at the top of the screen to upload a new one. Your photo appears in messages to teachers.

14.5 Attendance Goals

Go to Settings → Attendance Goals. Set a target attendance percentage for your child (e.g. 90%). The app will alert you when your child's attendance drops below your target.

15. Drive Cleanup Notice — What to Do

Occasionally, the school runs a Google Drive cleanup to free up storage space. When this happens, you will receive a flash message popup telling you that photos and videos will be permanently deleted from Drive on a specific date.

Act before the deadline — files cannot be recovered after deletion

Once the school deletes files from Google Drive, they are permanently gone. No one can recover them — not the school, not the admin, not Google. Download any photos or videos you want to keep before the date shown in the notice.

15.1 How to Download Your Child's Photos

1
The flash message popup includes a link to your class's Google Drive folder. Tap the link to open it.
2
In Google Drive, you will see folders organised by date (e.g. "2025-10-15/Photos"). Open each folder to see the photos.
3
Long-press any photo and tap Select All, then tap the download button to save to your Google Photos or phone storage.
4
On desktop: right-click → Download to download individual files, or select all and download as ZIP.
Also download from the Oakit feed

You can also download photos directly from the Oakit Class Feed while they are still visible (before the feed retention period expires). Go to Home → tap a photo → download icon. This is faster than going to Google Drive.

15.2 After the Cleanup

After the cleanup date, feed posts will still be visible in the Oakit app (the text, likes, and comments stay) but their photos and videos will no longer load. This is expected — the files have been removed from Drive. The posts themselves expire after the school's retention period (usually 20–30 days) and disappear from the feed entirely.

16. Instagram Follow Prompt

When you first open the app, you see a prompt asking you to follow the school's Instagram page. Here is why the school asks and what happens when you follow:

  • The school posts reels and carousel photos of classroom activities on Instagram
  • When you follow, you see these posts in your Instagram feed
  • Your follow helps the school's page grow, which helps them reach other parents who might want to enrol their children
  • Tap Open Instagram in the popup to go directly to the school's profile
  • Tap I have followed after following — the popup will not appear again
  • If you tap Remind me later, the popup appears again on your next login

17. Privacy and Data Safety

Your child's information and your family's data are protected in Oakit.

QuestionAnswer
Can other parents see my child's data?No. Each parent account is linked only to their own children. No other parent can see your child's attendance, fees, or messages.
Can teachers see my fee balance?No. Teachers do not have access to fee records.
Who can see my messages to the teacher?The teacher and the school admin can see messages. This ensures transparency and child safety.
Are photos shared with other schools?No. Photos are stored in your school's private Google Drive account and are not shared with any other school or organisation.
Can I request data deletion?Contact the school admin. They can deactivate your account and remove your data from the system.
Is my mobile number private?Your mobile number is your login ID and is known to the school admin. It is not shared with other parents.
