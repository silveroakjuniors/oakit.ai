1. What is Oakit.ai and What Can an Admin Do?

Oakit.ai is an AI-powered school management platform. It connects teachers, parents, and school leadership in one place, with Oakie (the AI assistant) helping teachers plan lessons and communicate with parents.

As an Admin, you have full control over everything within your school. You are the only person who can configure the platform, approve teacher content, manage fees, and maintain the system.

TeachersAccounts, section assignments, password resets

FeesFee structures, collections, concessions, invoices

2. Logging In

1
Open https://oakit.silveroakjuniors.in in any browser.
2
Enter your mobile number and password, then tap Sign In.
3
The system automatically redirects you to the Admin portal.
If you are locked out

Contact your Oakit system administrator or Franchise Admin to reset your password. Only a Super Admin can reset an admin account password directly.

Session & Security

Sessions expire after 8 hours of inactivity. If another device logs into your account, you will be signed out automatically (cross-tab session replacement). Never share your login credentials.

4. Student Management

4.1 Adding a New Student

1
Go to Admin → Students.
2
Click Add Student. Fill in name, date of birth, section, and parent contact numbers.
3
Click Save. The student is now enrolled.
Bulk import

To add many students at once, download the Excel template from the Students page, fill it in, and upload it. The system validates each row and shows errors before importing.

4.2 Editing a Student

Click the student's name → Edit. You can change section, parent details, date of birth, and photo.

4.3 Uploading a Student Photo

Open the student profile and tap the photo area. Upload a JPG or PNG (max 5 MB). Photos appear in the parent app and teacher attendance view.

4.4 Deactivating a Student (when a child leaves)

Open the student profile → Deactivate. The student's data is retained for records but they are removed from active lists, attendance, and fee calculations.

Do not delete — deactivate

Deleting a student removes all their historical data including attendance, fee records, and progress. Always deactivate instead.

7. Curriculum Management

7.1 Uploading a Textbook / Curriculum PDF

1
Go to Admin → Curriculum → Upload PDF.
2
Select the class / subject and upload the PDF. The file size limit is 50 MB.
3
Oakit's AI automatically extracts topics, chunks them, and indexes them for Oakie's lesson planning.
4
Indexing takes 2–5 minutes. You will see a "Processing" status until it's ready.

7.2 Monitoring Coverage

Go to Admin → Coverage. You can see per-section coverage percentages updated in real time as teachers mark topics as covered. Sections below 50% are highlighted on the dashboard.

When to upload curriculum

Upload all textbooks at the start of each academic year before teachers begin planning. Mid-year uploads work but teachers may need to re-plan sections that were already in progress.

8. Class Feed & Media

The Class Feed is where teachers post daily photos and videos of classroom activities. Parents see these in the Oakit parent app. All media is stored in Google Drive.

8.1 Feed Settings

Go to Admin → Settings → Feed Settings. You can configure:

  • Daily post limit per section — prevents teachers from flooding the feed (recommended: 5)
  • School-wide daily limit — total posts per day across all sections
  • Retention days — how long posts remain visible in the feed before auto-expiring (recommended: 20–30 days)
Why set retention days

Feed posts are not meant to be permanent galleries. 20–30 days is enough for parents to see recent activity. Shorter retention keeps the feed fresh and reduces storage. For long-term memories, teachers should use the student portfolio (notes and observations), not the feed.

8.2 Managing Feed Posts

Admins can delete any feed post. Go to Admin → Feed and click the delete icon on any post. Use this if inappropriate content is posted or if a parent requests removal.

9. Social Media Publisher

The Social Media Publisher lets teachers submit Instagram and Facebook posts for your approval. Nothing goes live without your sign-off.

Module activation

This module must be enabled by your Franchise Admin or Super Admin. Once enabled, go to Admin → Social Media to set up platform credentials.

9.1 Platform Setup (do this once)

1
Go to Admin → Social Media → Platform Setup.
2
For Instagram: enter your Page Access Token, Instagram Business Account ID, Facebook Page ID, and handle. You can get these from the Meta Developer Console. Follow the step-by-step guide shown on the page.
3
For Facebook: enter your Page Access Token and Page ID.
4
Click Test Connection to verify credentials are working.
5
Also enter your Meta App ID and App Secret so tokens can auto-refresh every 60 days.
Token expiry warning

Meta (Instagram/Facebook) access tokens expire every 60 days. The platform will warn you 7 days before expiry. Click Refresh Token to extend it immediately. If the token expires, all teacher posts will fail to publish.

9.2 Approval Queue — Your Daily Task

When teachers submit posts, you see a red badge on the Social Media tab. This is your most time-sensitive task.

1
Go to Admin → Social Media. The page opens on Approval Queue by default.
2
Review the photo/video, caption, and selected platforms for each post.
3
Click Approve & Publish to send it live immediately, or Reject with a reason if changes are needed.
4
Approved posts publish within 30–60 seconds. The teacher receives an in-app notification.
Bulk approve

Select multiple posts using the checkboxes and click Approve all to publish them all at once. Use this when you have many posts queued at the end of the day.

9.3 Post History — What to Check and When

Go to Admin → Social Media → Post History.

StatusMeaningAction

Retrying Failed Posts

If a post failed because of an expired token or a temporary API error, the error message tells you what went wrong. After fixing the credential (e.g. refreshing the token), click Retry Failed Platforms on the post card. The system retries only the platforms that failed.

Media deleted warning

If the post shows "Media files were deleted — ask teacher to resubmit", the Drive file was deleted before retry was possible. The teacher must upload and submit the post again.

9.4 Deleting a Published Post from Instagram/Facebook

If a post needs to be removed from social media after publishing, go to Post History, find the post, and click the red Delete post button next to the platform badge. This calls the Graph API and removes it from Instagram or Facebook directly.

9.5 Caption Best Practices

  • Oakie generates captions automatically — always include the teacher's notes so the AI has context
  • Every caption includes #yippppppp and 20 reach-boosting hashtags automatically appended at publish time
  • Total hashtags are capped at 28 to stay within Instagram's 30-hashtag limit
  • Captions are capped at 2,200 characters (Instagram's limit)

10. Google Drive Bulk Cleanup

Every photo, video, and file uploaded by teachers is stored in your school's Google Drive. Over time this fills up Drive storage. The Drive Cleanup tool lets you delete all old files in bulk after notifying parents first.

What gets deleted: All teacher-uploaded files older than your chosen cutoff — class feed photos/videos, teacher media, social media post files, student notes, and homework files.

10.1 When to run a cleanup

  • At the end of each term (every 3–4 months)
  • When Google Drive shows less than 20% free space
  • Before the start of a new academic year

10.2 Step-by-step cleanup process

Important — notify parents first

Parents cannot recover files after deletion. Always run Step 2 (Notify) and give parents at least 7 days to download before running Step 3 (Delete).

1
Go to Admin → Settings → Google Drive Cleanup (or Admin → Drive Cleanup directly).
2
Set "Delete files older than" to 30 days (or your preferred number, minimum 7).
3
Set "Notify parents (days before delete)" to at least 7.
4
Click Preview files to delete. The system shows you exactly how many files, their size, and which classes are affected.
5
Click Notify parents. This immediately sends a popup to all affected parents with:
  • A warning that files will be deleted on a specific date
  • A link directly to the class Google Drive folder
  • Instructions to download before the deadline
6
Wait the notification period (7+ days).
7
Return to the cleanup page, type DELETE in the confirmation box, and click Delete files permanently.
What happens to the Oakit feed after cleanup

Feed posts remain visible in the parent app (text, comments, likes) but their photos/videos will no longer load. The posts expire naturally based on the retention period you set. This is expected behaviour.

17. Calendar Setup, Special Days & the Lesson Planner — Complete Guide

This is the most powerful and most misunderstood part of Oakit. Take 10 minutes to read this section fully. It explains how the system plans lessons, what happens when you add a holiday after plans are generated, and how to fix anything that goes wrong.

17.1 How the Planner Works — The Core Logic

When you generate plans for a class, Oakit does the following:

  1. Looks at the academic calendar (start date, end date, working days)
  2. Removes all holidays and full-day special events from the working day count
  3. Takes the curriculum chunks (topics) for that class
  4. Distributes them one per working day, in order
  5. If a day gets cleared (holiday added later), the chunk from that day carries forward to the next available working day — no topic is ever lost
Key principle — carry-forward

Oakit never deletes a topic. If Day 15 becomes a holiday, Day 15's topic shifts to Day 16, Day 16's topic shifts to Day 17, and so on. The plan compresses intelligently. This is why you must refresh the planner after adding any holiday or special day.

17.2 Setting Up the Academic Calendar (do this once per year)

1
Go to Admin → Calendar.
2
Click New Calendar. Enter the academic year (e.g. 2025-2026), start date, and end date.
3
Select the working days — tick Monday through Friday (or whatever your school follows). This tells the system which days of the week are school days.
4
Click Save Calendar.
Do this before uploading curriculum

The academic calendar must exist before you generate plans. Curriculum chunks cannot be distributed until the system knows when school starts, ends, and which days are working days.

17.3 Holidays vs Special Days — What is the Difference?

TypeWhat it meansEffect on the planExample
Holiday School is completely closed. No teachers, no students. That day is removed entirely from the plan. Its topic carries forward. Diwali, Independence Day, sudden government holiday
Special Day — Full Day School is open but no curriculum teaching happens. The full day is used for an event or activity. That day is marked as a special event. Its topic carries forward. The day shows the event name instead of a topic. Annual Day, Sports Day, Parent-Teacher Meeting (full day)
Special Day — Half Day School is open but only half the day is used for curriculum. The other half has an activity/event. That day counts as 0.5 curriculum days. The topic stays but the teacher gets an instruction note. No carry-forward. Fancy Dress competition in the morning, curriculum in afternoon
Why half-day events exist

A half-day special day lets the teacher know they have less time than usual, but still need to cover the topic. For example: "Sports Day preparation 9–11 AM. Regular class after 11 AM." The topic stays assigned; the teacher just covers it in less time. The net curriculum days count is reduced by 0.5.

17.4 Adding a Holiday

1
Go to Admin → Calendar → select your academic year.
2
Click Add Holiday. Enter the date and event name (e.g. "Republic Day", "Sudden School Closure").
3
Click Save.
4
If plans have already been generated for that date, you will see a yellow warning: "X section plans were cleared. Those topics will carry forward once you refresh the planner."
5
Select the affected class and click Refresh Planner. The system redistributes the topics. Done.
Always refresh after adding a holiday

Adding a holiday clears the plan for that day but does NOT automatically shift all subsequent topics. You must click Refresh Planner for each affected class. If you skip this step, teachers will see a blank day followed by a gap in the sequence.

17.5 Adding a Special Day (School Event)

1
Go to Admin → Calendar → Special Days tab.
2
Click Add Special Day.
3
Fill in:
  • From date / To date — single day or multi-day event (e.g. 3-day cultural festival)
  • Event type — select from the list: Settling In, Sports Day, Cultural Event, Revision Day, Parent-Teacher Meeting, or Custom
  • Label — the display name teachers and parents see (e.g. "Annual Sports Day")
  • Duration type — Full Day or Half Day (see the table above)
  • Activity note — instructions for the teacher (optional, e.g. "Bring students to ground at 9 AM")
  • Start time / End time — for half-day events, specify when the event runs
4
Click Save. For full-day events, you will see the same carry-forward warning as holidays. Click Refresh Planner.

17.6 Special Day Types — What Each Means

TypeWhen to use it
Settling InFirst few days of school year — children are adjusting. No formal curriculum yet. Teachers do welcome activities.
Sports DaySchool athletic event. Teachers go to the ground, not the classroom.
Cultural Event / Annual DayPerformance, concert, prize ceremony.
Revision DayA full day dedicated to revision of previously taught topics. When you add this type, you can specify which topics to revise — Oakie will suggest relevant questions.
Parent-Teacher MeetingAdmin marks these so teachers know to prepare reports. Curriculum paused for the day or half-day.
CustomAnything else — Republic Day programme, field trip, health check day.

17.7 Generating Monthly and Yearly Plans

1
Go to Admin → Plans.
2
Select the Class and Section.
3
Choose Generate Month Plans (for a single month) or Generate Full Year Plans (for all remaining months).
4
The system distributes curriculum chunks across working days, skipping holidays and full-day special events automatically.
5
Once generated, teachers immediately see their daily plans in the Teacher → Plan tab.
Generate month-by-month vs full year

Full year generation is faster but locks in all plans at once. Month-by-month gives you flexibility — if December has an unexpected event, you only need to regenerate December, not the whole year. Recommended: generate month-by-month, one month at a time, at the start of each month.

17.8 Regenerating Plans — When You Need to Delete and Regenerate

Use Refresh Planner after adding a holiday or special day to an already-generated month. This shifts topics forward without deleting them.

Use Delete All Plans + Generate only when you need to completely redo a month — for example if you uploaded new curriculum, changed working days, or the month is severely out of sequence.

Important — you cannot regenerate a month that already has plans

The system prevents overwriting existing plans to avoid teachers losing their work. To regenerate:

  1. Go to Admin → Plans → select the class and month
  2. Click Delete All Plans for that month
  3. Then click Generate Month Plans
Teachers will not lose their completion records — only the plan distribution resets.

17.9 Editing a Day Plan — Adding a Note or Changing a Topic Label

Sometimes you want to adjust a specific day without regenerating everything. Go to Admin → Plans, click on any day, and you can:

  • Admin Note — add a note visible only to the teacher for that day (e.g. "Use the new chart from the storeroom today"). This appears as a blue notice box in the teacher's plan.
  • Edit topic label — rename the topic for that day without affecting the curriculum database (the AI still uses the original topic internally).
  • Add an extra topic or activity — if you want to insert an additional topic for a specific day that is not in the uploaded curriculum (e.g. a school-specific activity), use the Admin Note field to instruct the teacher. The raw curriculum chunks cannot be injected mid-plan without regenerating — but the admin note achieves the same result for one-off additions.
How to add a topic not in the curriculum

If the school wants to add a topic that is not in the uploaded textbook (e.g. a special environmental awareness lesson):

  1. Go to Admin → Plans → find the target day
  2. Click the day → Add Admin Note
  3. Write: "Today's additional topic: Environmental Awareness — discuss waste sorting, recycling, and conservation with the class."
  4. Save. The teacher sees this note highlighted in blue on their daily plan.
For a permanent addition to the curriculum, upload a new PDF or add the topic to the existing curriculum source and re-ingest.

17.10 The Coverage Report — How to Read It

Go to Admin → Coverage. This shows how much of the curriculum each section has actually completed (not just planned).

ColumnMeaning
SectionClass name and section label
Total chunksTotal curriculum topics for that class
CoveredTopics marked as done by the teacher
Coverage %Covered ÷ Total × 100
FlaggedAdmin or principal has flagged this section for attention
Coverage vs Plans

Plans tell you what was scheduled. Coverage tells you what was actually taught. A section can have 100% plans generated but only 40% coverage if teachers are behind. Coverage is the real metric.

17.11 Carry-Forward Scenario — Step by Step

Here is a concrete example so you understand exactly what happens:

Scenario: Sudden holiday on Wednesday, 15 January

The plan before the holiday:

  • Mon 13 Jan — Topic: "Numbers 1–10"
  • Tue 14 Jan — Topic: "Addition basics"
  • Wed 15 Jan — Topic: "Subtraction intro" ← You add a holiday here
  • Thu 16 Jan — Topic: "Shapes"
  • Fri 17 Jan — Topic: "Colours"

After you add the holiday and click Refresh Planner:

  • Mon 13 Jan — Topic: "Numbers 1–10" (unchanged)
  • Tue 14 Jan — Topic: "Addition basics" (unchanged)
  • Wed 15 Jan — Holiday (no topic)
  • Thu 16 Jan — Topic: "Subtraction intro" (shifted from Wed)
  • Fri 17 Jan — Topic: "Shapes" (shifted from Thu)
  • Mon 20 Jan — Topic: "Colours" (shifted from Fri)

Every topic is preserved. The plan simply extends by one day.

18. Principal — What You Can Do

The principal role has visibility across the entire school and can do almost everything the admin can do, except manage fees, HR salary records, and school settings. The principal's focus is on academic oversight, teacher performance, and content approval.

18.1 Principal Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:

  • School health charts — attendance rate, coverage rate, teacher engagement
  • Sections with low coverage (flagged automatically below 50%)
  • Pending approvals — leave requests, fee concessions waiting for action
  • Teacher streaks — which teachers have been consistently active
  • Birthday list — students and staff with upcoming birthdays
  • Finance snapshot (read-only)

18.2 Social Media — Full Approval Access

The principal has identical access to the Social Media Publisher as the admin:

  • Approve or reject posts in the approval queue
  • Edit captions before approving
  • View post history, retry failed platforms, delete published posts from Instagram/Facebook
  • Configure platform credentials
  • Run Drive cleanup for social media files

Access: Principal Dashboard → Social Media card (shown on the dashboard) or navigate directly to /principal/social-media.

18.3 Coverage and Planner Oversight

The principal can view coverage reports for all classes and sections. If a section is falling behind, the principal can:

  • View the specific topics not yet covered
  • Check teacher activity logs
  • Go to Admin → Plans (accessible to principal) to add an admin note to upcoming days with specific instructions
  • Flag a section for follow-up

18.4 Teacher Performance

Go to Principal → Teacher Activity. This shows each teacher's:

  • Number of days they marked attendance
  • Number of feed posts created
  • Curriculum coverage percentage for their sections
  • Streak (consecutive days of activity)

Use this to identify teachers who are consistently not using the platform, then have a conversation with them.

18.5 Announcements

The principal can create school-wide and class-specific announcements, the same as the admin.

18.6 Leave Approval

The principal can approve or reject staff leave requests from Principal Dashboard → Leave.

18.7 Holistic Reports

The principal can view all student holistic reports generated by teachers, and can download them as PDFs for record-keeping or parent meetings.

19. Teacher — What You Can Do

The teacher role is the core daily user of Oakit. Everything a teacher does feeds into the parent's view and the admin's oversight.

19.1 Teacher Home — The Daily Workflow

When a teacher logs in, they see:

  • Today's plan — the topic assigned for today, with Oakie's suggested activities
  • Attendance section — mark who is present, absent, or late with one tap
  • Pending topics — topics from previous days that were not completed and carried forward
  • Admin note (if any) — a blue box with instructions from the admin for today

19.2 Marking Attendance

1
Open the app → today's view shows the class list.
2
Tap each student's name to mark Present, Absent, or Late.
3
Tap Submit Attendance. Parents are notified immediately if their child is absent.
Mark attendance before 10 AM

Parents check the app first thing in the morning. Late attendance marking causes parents to contact the school unnecessarily. Train teachers to mark attendance by 9:30 AM every day.

19.3 Completing a Topic (Plan Tab)

1
Go to Plan tab → find today's topic.
2
Tick the checkbox next to the topic after teaching it.
3
The topic moves to "Completed". Coverage percentage updates on the admin's dashboard immediately.

If the teacher does not tick a topic, Oakit automatically carries it forward to the next day. The teacher will see it in the "Pending" section tomorrow.

19.4 Posting to the Class Feed

1
Go to Feed tab → tap New Post.
2
Upload 1–5 photos, or one video (max 90 seconds). Videos over 15 MB are auto-compressed.
3
Write a caption or tap Generate with Oakie for an AI caption.
4
Post. Parents of that section see it immediately in their app.

19.5 Homework and Notes

  • Assign homework: Teacher → Homework → New. Write the task, set a due date. Parents see it in the app. Parents tick "acknowledged" when they have seen it.
  • Class notes: Teacher → Notes → New Note. Attach files or write text. Notes auto-expire after the number of days configured by admin (default 14 days).

19.6 Oakie AI Chat

Teachers can ask Oakie anything from the chat tab:

  • "What activities can I do for the topic Numbers 1–10 for Nursery kids?"
  • "Write a homework task for today's lesson on colours."
  • "Suggest a 5-minute warm-up activity for a Monday morning class."
  • "What topics has my class not covered yet?"

Oakie knows the class's curriculum because it was indexed from the uploaded PDF. Every response can be downloaded as a PDF.

19.7 Social Media Posts (if module is enabled)

1
Go to Social Media in the teacher app.
2
Select Video/Reel or Photo Carousel (carousel requires at least 2 photos).
3
Upload media. Write a caption or use Oakie to generate one.
4
Select platforms (Instagram, Facebook, etc.) and tap Submit for Approval.
5
The admin/principal reviews and approves. The teacher gets a notification when published.
6
In My Posts, the teacher can see the status of each post and a direct link to view it on Instagram/Facebook after it is published.
Daily limits

Teachers can submit 1 video and 2 photo carousels per day. Failed posts (not published due to errors) do not count against this limit. Posts that were deleted by the teacher also do not count — the slot is freed.

19.8 Milestones and Observations

Go to Teacher → Milestones. Mark developmental milestones for each student — physical, cognitive, social, and emotional. Write observations. These feed into the holistic term report generated by Oakie at the end of term.

19.9 Teacher Handout (PDF)

Go to Teacher → Download Handout. This generates a full weekly or monthly PDF plan for the teacher to keep at their desk or share with the principal. It includes every day's topic, activities, and special day notes.

20. Parent — What You Can See and Do

The parent portal is designed to keep parents informed without overwhelming them. It is intentionally read-focused — parents cannot change curriculum, attendance, or class content. They can communicate with teachers and download their child's materials.

20.1 What Parents See on Login

  • Instagram follow prompt — a one-time popup asking them to follow the school's Instagram page
  • Flash message — if the admin has sent an urgent notice (e.g. sudden holiday, Drive cleanup warning), it appears as a full-screen popup
  • Home feed — today's class photo/video posts from the teacher

20.2 Home Tab

  • Today's class feed posts (photos and videos)
  • Today's attendance status for their child
  • Today's curriculum topic (what was planned for teaching)
  • Homework due today

20.3 Attendance Tab

Parents can see a 30-day attendance history for their child — present, absent, late. The attendance percentage is shown clearly.

20.4 Progress Tab

Parents see the curriculum coverage percentage for their child's class — how much of the textbook has been taught so far. This is the same metric the admin and principal see.

20.5 Homework Tab

All homework assigned by the teacher. Parents tap Acknowledge once they have seen it and ensured their child has done it. The teacher sees who has acknowledged.

20.6 Messages Tab

Direct messaging between the parent and the class teacher. Parents can ask questions about their child's progress, attendance, or homework. All messages are logged and visible to the admin.

20.7 Milestones Tab

Parents can see developmental milestones marked by the teacher. When new milestones are added, the parent receives a notification.

20.8 Fees Tab

Parents can view their child's fee invoice, payment history, and outstanding balance. They cannot make payments through the app — payments are recorded by the admin after receiving cash/online transfer.

20.9 Reports Tab

End-of-term holistic reports generated by Oakie are available here as downloadable PDFs. Parents can also see the student journey (a timeline of milestones and observations through the year).

20.10 Multi-Language Support

Parents can enable translation in Settings. The app supports Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil, Malayalam, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Punjabi, Urdu, Arabic, French, and Spanish. When enabled, all teacher-written content is automatically translated for that parent.

20.11 What Parents CANNOT Do

  • Mark attendance
  • Edit homework or curriculum
  • See other children's data (each parent only sees their linked children)
  • Access teacher or admin sections
  • Make fee payments through the app
