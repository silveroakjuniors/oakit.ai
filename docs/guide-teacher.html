Role: Teacher  —  Manage your class, plan lessons, communicate with parents

1. What Teachers Can Do

As a teacher in Oakit, you manage your assigned sections day-to-day. You are the primary person parents interact with through the platform, and your activity directly affects what parents see in their app.

2. Logging In

1
Open https://oakit.silveroakjuniors.in in Chrome or Safari on your phone or computer.
2
Enter your mobile number and password. Tap Sign In.
3
The app opens on your Teacher portal. You will see only your assigned sections.
Install the app on your phone

The Oakit app works as a PWA (Progressive Web App). In Chrome on Android: tap the three-dot menu → "Add to Home Screen". On iPhone Safari: tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen". The app icon appears on your home screen and works offline for reading.

Forgot your password

Contact your admin. Only the admin can reset teacher passwords. The admin will give you a temporary password to log in with, then you can change it in Settings → Change Password.

4. Marking Attendance

1
Open the app. Tap Attendance at the bottom of the screen.
2
You will see today's class list for your section. Tap each student to mark Present, Absent, or Late.
3
Tap Submit Attendance.
4
Parents of absent students receive an in-app notification immediately after submission.
Mark attendance every day — no exceptions

If attendance is not marked, parents see "Not marked yet" in their app and assume the teacher is absent or the system is broken. Unmarked attendance also breaks the monthly attendance reports that parents and admin rely on.

4.1 Marking Late Arrivals

If a student arrives after attendance has been submitted, re-open the attendance view and change their status to "Late". You can update attendance until end of day. The timestamp of the late arrival is recorded.

4.2 If You Made a Mistake

You can re-open today's attendance at any time before midnight and correct it. Changes are logged.

4.3 Viewing Previous Days' Attendance

You can now go back and view attendance for any past date — this was a commonly requested feature.

1
Open the Attendance page. At the top you will see a date navigator with left/right arrow buttons.
2
Tap the ← arrow to go to the previous day, or use the date picker to jump directly to any past date.
3
The attendance for that day loads — Present/Absent status is shown for every student. Past days are read-only.
4
Tap the Today button at any time to return to today's attendance.
Why view past attendance

Use this to verify what was submitted on a specific day, resolve parent queries about absence records, or check patterns before a parent-teacher meeting.

4.4 Per-Student Absence History

You can see every date a specific student was absent this academic year — including their overall attendance percentage.

1
On any past day's attendance view (or after submitting today's attendance), tap any student's name row.
2
A panel slides up showing:
  • Total school days, present count, absent count
  • Attendance percentage with a colour bar (green above 75%, red below)
  • A warning if the student is below 75% — the standard threshold for concern
  • Full list of every absent date this year, newest first
  • Late arrival dates are marked with a "Late" badge
3
Tap the X to close and return to the class list.
Use this before parent-teacher meetings

Open the absence history for each student the day before PTM. If a student is below 75% attendance, you can raise it with the parent directly with specific dates as evidence. This eliminates the need to maintain a paper register or count absences manually.

5. Today's Plan — Reading and Using It

Go to the Plan tab. This shows today's lesson plan generated by the admin.

5.1 What You Will See

  • Topic card — the curriculum topic assigned for today, with Oakie's suggested activities for that topic
  • Admin note (blue box) — special instructions from the admin for today (e.g. "Use the chart from the storeroom today" or "PTM preparation — keep today's class shorter"). Read this first.
  • Pending topics — topics from previous days that you did not tick as complete. These carry forward automatically and appear here until you complete them.
  • Special day label — if today is a special event (Sports Day, PTM, etc.), the event name appears instead of or alongside the topic.
What "pending topics" means

If you did not tick a topic yesterday, Oakit did not delete it. It moved to today as a pending item. This is called "carry-forward". You can see pending topics at the top of the Plan tab. Complete them when you can — they are tracked in the coverage report.

5.2 Viewing Future Plans

Tap the arrows on the Plan tab to navigate forward through the week or month. You can see what topics are coming up so you can prepare materials in advance.

5.3 If the Plan Shows "No Plan"

This means the admin has not yet generated plans for this month. Contact your admin. The admin generates plans from Admin → Plans.

6. Completing a Topic

1
Go to Plan tab → find today's topic card.
2
After teaching the topic, tap the checkbox on the topic card.
3
The topic moves to "Completed". The class's coverage percentage updates immediately on the admin's dashboard.
What if you could not complete the topic today

That is fine — do not tick it. Oakit automatically carries it forward to tomorrow. It will appear in your pending list. You will see it again the next day without any action needed from you.

Why ticking matters

When you tick a topic, three things happen: (1) the admin's coverage report updates, (2) parents see that the topic was taught today, and (3) the topic is removed from the pending list. Not ticking means the admin and principal think you are behind on curriculum — even if you taught the topic in class.

7. Posting to the Class Feed

The Class Feed is the daily photo/video diary of your classroom. Parents see these posts in real time in their app. This is the most engaging part of Oakit for parents — post at least once every day.

1
Go to the Feed tab → tap New Post (or the camera icon).
2
Upload 1–5 photos or one video. Tap the upload area and select files from your phone.
3
Write a caption describing what is happening in the photos, or tap Generate with Oakie to get an AI-written caption.
4
Tap Post. Parents see it immediately.

7.1 Video Guidelines

  • Maximum video length: 90 seconds
  • Videos over 15 MB are automatically compressed before upload (you will see a progress bar)
  • If the video is longer than 90 seconds, trim it on your phone before uploading
  • Vertical (portrait) videos look best on phones

7.2 Photo Guidelines

  • Up to 5 photos per post
  • Formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP
  • Avoid blurry photos — parents want to see their child clearly
  • Group activities, art projects, and class moments make the best posts

7.3 Daily Post Limit

Your admin has set a daily limit for posts per section (usually 5). If you reach the limit, you cannot post more until tomorrow. Focus on quality over quantity.

7.4 Deleting a Post

Go to your post in the feed → tap the three-dot menu → Delete. Only your own posts can be deleted by you. The admin can delete any post.

8. Homework

8.1 Assigning Homework

1
Go to Homework tab → Assign Homework.
2
Write the homework task. Be specific: "Complete page 15, questions 1–5 in the Maths workbook."
3
Set the due date.
4
Optionally tap Suggest with Oakie — Oakie will suggest homework based on today's topic.
5
Tap Assign. All parents of your section see it immediately.

8.2 Tracking Parent Acknowledgements

Go to Homework → select the homework. You can see which parents have tapped "Acknowledged" and which have not. Use this to follow up with parents who haven't seen the homework.

Why acknowledging matters

When a parent taps "Acknowledged", they confirm they have seen the homework and ensured their child will do it. This is a digital record replacing the physical homework diary. It is much more reliable — the parent's acknowledgement is timestamped.

9. Class Notes

Class notes are messages sent to all parents of your section — class reminders, event information, materials, or anything parents need to know that is not homework.

1
Go to Notes tab → New Note.
2
Write your note. Optionally attach a file (PDF, image, Word document).
3
Set an expiry date (or leave as the default set by admin, usually 14 days).
4
Tap Send Note. Parents see it in their Updates / Notes section.
Notes auto-expire

Notes disappear after their expiry date. This keeps the parent's view clean. If you need a permanent announcement (e.g. school calendar), ask the admin to post it as an Announcement instead.

10. Oakie AI Chat

Oakie is your AI teaching assistant. It knows your class's curriculum because the admin uploaded the PDF textbooks, and Oakie read and indexed them. You can ask Oakie anything related to teaching and your class.

10.1 How to Use Oakie

1
Tap the Oakie tab (chat bubble icon) or the floating Oakie button.
2
Type your question or use the microphone for voice input.
3
Oakie responds with text, activity ideas, or structured content.
4
Tap the Download PDF button to save any Oakie response as a PDF file.

10.2 Example Questions to Ask Oakie

What you wantWhat to type
Activity ideas for a topic"What hands-on activities can I do with Nursery students for the topic 'Animals'?"
Homework suggestion"Suggest a simple homework task for today's lesson on colours for 3-year-olds."
Class management help"How do I handle a child who refuses to participate in group activities?"
Warm-up activity"Give me a 5-minute warm-up activity for a Monday morning KG class."
Story or rhyme"Tell me a short story I can read aloud about friendship for Nursery children."
What topics are due"What topics has my class not covered yet this month?"
Assessment ideas"How can I assess if my Nursery students understand the concept of big and small?"
Oakie knows your curriculum

When you ask Oakie about activities for a specific topic, it cross-references the uploaded curriculum PDF for your class. This means suggestions are aligned with what you are actually supposed to be teaching — not generic content.

10.3 Downloading Oakie Responses as PDF

Every Oakie response has a small PDF download button. Tap it to save the content as a formatted PDF. Use this to: print activity sheets, share content with the principal, or build a teaching folder.

AI credits

Each school has a quota of AI queries. If you see "out of credits" error, contact your admin. The admin can top up AI credits from Settings.

11. Social Media Posts

If your school has the Social Media Publisher enabled, you can submit Instagram/Facebook posts for the admin's approval. Nothing goes live without admin sign-off.

11.1 Submitting a Post

1
Go to Social Media tab → New Post.
2
Select Video/Reel or Photo Carousel. For carousel, you must upload at least 2 photos (maximum 5).
3
Upload your media. Videos auto-compress if over 15 MB. Maximum video length: 90 seconds.
4
Write a caption (your notes about the post) or tap Generate with Oakie for a viral-optimised caption with hashtags.
5
Select the platforms (Instagram, Facebook, etc.) and tap Submit for Approval.
6
The post is in the admin's approval queue. You receive an in-app notification when it is approved and published.

11.2 Daily Limits

  • 1 video per day
  • 2 photo carousels per day
  • Deleted posts free up the slot — if you delete a post, you can submit again
  • Failed posts (not published due to errors) also do not count against the limit

11.3 Managing Your Posts (My Posts Tab)

StatusWhat it meansWhat you can do
pending approvalWaiting for admin to reviewWait. Contact admin if urgent.
publishedLive on social media. Shows "View on Instagram/Facebook" link.Tap the link to see your live post
rejectedAdmin returned it with a reasonRead the rejection reason, edit your caption or media, and resubmit
failedPublishing failed (platform error)Wait for admin to retry. The error was a technical issue, not your fault.
recalledYou recalled it before approvalEdit and resubmit, or delete

11.4 Recalling a Post

You can recall a post that is in rejected or failed status. This moves it back to your draft state so you can edit and resubmit. You cannot recall a post that is pending approval or already published.

11.5 Viewing Your Published Post

In My Posts, find a published post. You will see "View on Instagram" or "View on Facebook" links next to the platform badge. Tap to open the live post directly in Instagram or Facebook.

12. Milestones and Observations

Milestones are developmental achievements you mark for each student. They cover four areas: cognitive, physical, social, and emotional development. This is the digital replacement for the developmental checklist teachers normally do on paper.

1
Go to Milestones tab → select a student.
2
You will see a checklist of milestones for the class level. Tick each milestone when the child achieves it.
3
Tap Add Observation to write a free-text observation (e.g. "Priya showed excellent problem-solving during the blocks activity today").
4
Save. Parents see newly achieved milestones in their app. Observations are visible to parents and admin.
Why record milestones weekly

Milestones feed directly into the AI-generated holistic term report. The more milestones and observations you record, the richer and more personalised the report will be. Parents treasure these reports.

13. Holistic Reports

At the end of each term, Oakie generates a personalised holistic development report for each student based on the milestones and observations you recorded.

1
Go to Reports tab → Generate Holistic Report.
2
Select the student and term.
3
Oakie generates a paragraph-format report covering all four development areas based on the recorded data.
4
Review the report. If you want to add or change anything, edit the text directly.
5
Tap Save Report. The parent can now see it in their Reports tab and download it as a PDF.
6
To regenerate (if you added more observations after generating), tap the Refresh button on the saved report.
Generate reports before parent-teacher meetings

Have holistic reports ready 2–3 days before PTM day so parents have time to read them. Reports are available to parents in the app immediately after you save them.

14. Observations

Observations are free-text notes about a specific student. They complement milestones by capturing context — the story behind the achievement.

  • Write at least one observation per student per week if possible
  • Be specific: "Arjun counted to 20 without help during the morning circle activity on Tuesday" is better than "Arjun did well in counting"
  • Observations are visible to the principal and admin, and to the parent in the student portfolio
  • They feed into the holistic AI report at term end

15. Teacher Handout PDF

Go to Plan tab → Download Handout. Select the week or month. The system generates a formatted PDF plan with every day's topic, activities, and special day notes — ready to print and keep at your desk, or share with the principal.

16. Voice Input

You can use your phone's microphone to talk to Oakie instead of typing. Tap the microphone icon in the Oakie chat box, speak your question, and Oakie will respond. This works well for quick questions during class when typing is inconvenient.

Voice in multiple languages

Voice input supports any language your phone recognises. If you are more comfortable in Hindi or Telugu, speak in that language and Oakie will understand and respond in English (or your preferred language if translation is enabled).

17. Settings

  • Change Password — go to Settings → Change Password. Use a strong password of at least 8 characters.
  • Notifications — enable or disable push notifications for different events (new messages, homework acknowledgements, etc.)
  • Language — change the app display language
  • Profile photo — upload a photo that appears in the teacher profile visible to parents
